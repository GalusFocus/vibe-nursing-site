What this app is

Vibe Nursing monitors publicly available nurse residency and new-graduate program pages and reports what it finds. Programs set their own requirements, deadlines, pay and cohort dates and can change them without notice. The posting on the program’s own site is always the final word. We show you where every fact came from and when we last read it precisely so that you can check us.

Practice questions

Questions are generated by an AI model from material you upload and from publicly published exam blueprints. They are practice in the style of those exams. They are not past papers, not licensed item banks, and not a prediction of your score. Verify against your course content and the official test plan, and flag anything that looks wrong. Every flagged item is re-checked against the source it cites, and pulled if it does not hold up.

Scrub, the assistant in the app, answers with the same kind of AI model and can be wrong. Check anything that matters against the program’s own page, your school or your instructor.

Nothing generated here is medical, clinical, legal or immigration advice, and none of it should be used to make a decision about a real patient.

What the plans actually change

Prep is free, and stays free. 10 new practice questions a day, from your own lecture slides or our question bank, with your misses to review, your streak, and joining a friend’s Study Together session. You never have to pay for that.

Everything else is Vibe Plus. Every residency program in all 50 states with its dates, requirements and pay; Track with countdowns; alerts about the programs you watch, within minutes of our finding them and outside the quiet hours you set; Ask Scrub; interview prep; hosting Study Together; and 100 questions a day.

One alert has a floor written into it. News about a program on your board that closes today or tomorrow is sent on every plan, including after a subscription ends. We are willing to sell speed. We are not willing to sell a deadline.

What we do not promise

We do not guarantee that you will be hired, interviewed or admitted anywhere, and no part of this subscription is contingent on any of those things happening. We also do not guarantee that every change to every program page reaches you. We read public pages and they can change in ways we do not see or that a site blocks us from reading. The posting on the program’s own site is always the final word, and the deadline on it is the one that counts.

Fair use of generation

Every generated question costs real money to produce, so each plan has a daily ceiling: 10 questions a day on the free plan and 100 a day on a paid one, shown to you inside the app before you reach it. We will tell you in the app before we lower either number, and a ceiling we have raised for a day is not a promise to keep it raised. Automated, scripted or shared-account use, and resale of generated questions, are not permitted and may result in an account being limited.

The program catalog is free to read and it is not free to take. Automated collection, scraping, bulk export or redistribution of the catalog, by any method and on any plan, is not permitted, and we rate-limit and monitor for it. The individual facts in it are public; the collection, the checking and the freshness are ours.

Your content

Lecture slides and notes you upload stay yours. You give us permission to process them for the sole purpose of generating your questions and tracking your recall. We do not use your uploads to train models and we do not share them with other users. The text of a file you upload, and a question you ask Scrub, go to our AI provider only after you allow it in the app, and you can take that back under You. Only upload material you are allowed to upload, and never upload anything containing real patient information.

Cancel or delete your account at any time from the You tab. We may suspend an account being used to abuse the service or harm other people. If we make a material change to these terms we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.

If you stop paying, you keep everything you made. Your board, your notes, your résumé, your interview notes and your questions are kept, and come back exactly as you left them when you subscribe again. Nothing you saved is ever deleted because a subscription ended. On the free plan you keep Prep, and the programs, Track, alerts, Scrub and interview prep wait for Plus. We will never hold a job search hostage to a renewal: the closing-tomorrow warning keeps coming either way.
