Privacy Policy

Last updated 20 October 2026

The short version

  • We never contact your employer or your school about your job search, and we never post anything anywhere.
  • We do not sell your data or your phone number. There is no advertising business here.
  • Your Apple, Google or phone sign-in is used to sign you in. Alerts come only if you switch them on.
  • Uploads and Scrub questions go to our AI provider only after you allow it. They are not training data.
  • Delete your account and everything goes within 30 days.

What we collect

How you sign in. With Apple or Google we receive an account identifier and, unless you hide it, an email address. With phone we store the number. We never require a legal name, a home address, a license number or a date of birth.

What you tell us. Your graduation stage, target states and specialties. These exist to filter the catalog down to the programs worth your attention, and nothing else.

What you do. Which programs you watch and where they are on your board, which questions you answer and how you did. This is what powers your vitals and your recall tracking.

How you use the app. A short, fixed list of actions, such as opening a program, saving one or moving it to a new stage, each with the time, your phone type, the app version and a code your phone makes at random the first time the app opens. Never your notes, what you search for, your name or your number. We use them to learn what helps and what does not, keep them for 400 days, and delete them along with your account.

If you switch alerts on. Your phone’s notification address, its time zone so that quiet hours fall at night where you are, your alert settings, and which alerts you opened, so that we stop pushing kinds you never open.

If you subscribe. Your plan, its renewal date and its status, as Apple or Google and our subscription service report them, and the name of a discount code if you used one. We never see your card or bank details.

What you upload and ask. Lecture files and the text extracted from them, a résumé if you add one, and the questions you ask Scrub.

If you join the waitlist on our website. Your email address, which button you used and the day. We use it only to tell you when the app is on the App Store. To stop a script pouring in addresses, we count tries from your connection for each hour, kept only as a scrambled code that changes every day and is deleted after two days, never as your network address.

What we never do

We do not contact employers, schools, recruiters or licensing boards about you. We do not sell or rent personal information. We do not run ads, and we do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not use your uploaded material to train AI models. We do not send marketing texts. If a message from us arrives, it is a sign-in code or an alert you switched on.

Who we share with, and why

Only the service providers it takes to run the app, each for one job. Our hosting provider runs our database, sign-in and server code. Our AI provider writes practice questions and Scrub’s answers, and receives the text of a file you upload or a question you ask only after you allow it in the app; it does not train its models on what we send. Apple and Google sign you in if you choose them, deliver notifications and take payment. Our subscription service keeps the record of your subscription. Our notification service passes our notifications to Apple and Google. A text-message provider will send sign-in codes once phone sign-in is switched on. Each receives the minimum it needs, may not use it for anything else, and must protect it as this policy does. For the current list of these providers, write to support@vibenursing.com.

How long we keep it

Uploaded files are kept while your account is open so you can regenerate from them, and you can delete any file yourself at any time. Delete your account and we remove your personal data within 30 days, apart from the minimum we must retain for tax and fraud records. A waitlist address is deleted once we have sent that email, or sooner if you write to support@vibenursing.com and ask.

Your controls

Delete your account and its data from the You tab, under Account & sign-in. Turn alerts, each kind of alert, and what may go to our AI provider on or off there too. For a copy of your data, or to correct it, contact us from Help & contact us. Depending on where you live you may also have the right to restrict how we use your data, or object. We honor those requests for everybody rather than only where the law requires it.

Children

Vibe Nursing is for nursing students and nurses and is not intended for anyone under 16.
