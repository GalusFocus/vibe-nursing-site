A person reads every message.

Deleting your account

In the app: You, then Account & sign-in, then Delete account. What is removed, and when, is in the Privacy Policy.

Cancelling a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. In the app: You, then Manage subscription. Or on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions.

Plans and billing

What is free, and what is Vibe Plus?

Prep is free: 10 new practice questions a day, from your own lecture slides or the question bank, with your misses to review and your streak. You can also join a friend’s Study Together session.

Vibe Plus adds every residency program in all 50 states with its dates, requirements and pay, Track with countdowns, alerts within minutes, Ask Scrub, interview prep, hosting Study Together, and 100 questions a day. It is $14.99 a month, $29.99 every 3 months or $83.99 a year.

Is there a free trial?

No. The free plan is how to try Vibe Nursing: Prep works every day with a daily limit, and Discover shows how many programs match your states before you subscribe. You can cancel Plus any time, and you keep it until the end of the period you paid for.

Cancelling or pausing Plus

Open You, then Manage subscription, or on your iPhone open Settings, your name, then Subscriptions. Apple and Google do not let an app cancel its own subscription, so the cancel button is always theirs.

You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for. After that you are on the free plan, with no end date, and nothing you made is deleted.

I paid on another phone. How do I get Plus back?

Sign in with the same account, then tap Restore at the bottom of the Vibe Plus screen, or Restore purchases under You. Plus follows your account and your Apple ID, so it is yours on every phone you sign in to.

How do I use a discount code?

Tap Have a code? on the Vibe Plus screen, or Redeem a code under You, and type the code into Apple’s sheet. If we sent you a link, tapping it fills the code in for you.

Apple applies the discount and shows you the price before you confirm, so you always see what you will pay.

Can I get a refund?

Payments go through Apple or Google, so refunds do too. On iPhone, ask at reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Vibe Nursing. We cannot issue a refund ourselves, but we are happy to help if something went wrong.

Programs and alerts

Where does the program information come from?

From each hospital’s own page. Our reader visits every program page in the catalog about twice a day, and a person on our team confirms the dates before a program shows as Open or Soon.

The program’s own page always wins. Check it before you apply, and if ours disagrees, tell us.

What do Open and Soon mean?

Open means our team confirmed the program is taking applications today. Soon means we confirmed an opening date still ahead. We never mark a program Open because we think it is due, and when the deadline passes it goes back to All by itself.

A program’s details look wrong

The program’s own page always wins. Tap See something wrong here? at the bottom of the program’s screen, pick what is off, and a person on our team reads it.

We would much rather you tell us than quietly stop trusting the app.

A program I know is missing

Search for it in Discover. When nothing matches, tap Know a program we are missing? and we add it once we have checked the hospital’s own page.

How do alerts work?

Watch a program with its bell, and Vibe Plus tells you when it opens, when a person on our team confirms new dates, when its page changes in a way that matters, and when it has new-graduate openings. News about a program closing today or tomorrow reaches you on every plan.

Under You, then Notifications, you choose quiet hours, how many alerts a day, and whether the rest arrive together in one evening summary.

I am not getting alerts

Check four things. Notifications are on for Vibe Nursing in your iPhone’s Settings. You are signed in. Alerts are on under You, then Notifications. And it is not inside your quiet hours, when alerts wait for the morning.

Alerts are part of Vibe Plus, apart from the closing-tomorrow warning. If all four are right and nothing arrives, write to us.

Practice and AI

How many practice questions do I get a day?

10 a day on the free plan and 100 a day on a paid one, both resetting once a day, and the app shows you when. Answering and reviewing questions you have already made is never metered on any plan, because the cap counts new questions only, so going back over yesterday’s misses always costs you nothing.

A cap exists because every new question costs us real money to generate, and a plan that cannot pay for its own heaviest user is a price list that is not true. The paid number is deliberately a ceiling we can afford to draw and would not want reached.

Where do the practice questions come from?

Two places. The question bank is written to the published test plans. Questions from your slides are written by an AI model from your own lecture, only after you allow it, and each one carries its rationale and the slide it came from.

They are practice in the style of the exams, not exam content. Flag anything that looks wrong and we re-check it against its source.

Is any of this medical advice?

No. Everything here is study material, and none of it should guide a decision about a real patient. Your instructor, your facility’s policies and the official test plan come first.

Account and privacy

Can my employer or school see any of this?

No. We never contact an employer, a school, a recruiter or a board about you, and nothing you do here is posted anywhere.

If you would rather not link a work Google account at all, you can sign in with Apple and hide your email instead. That is exactly why we offer it.

What does Scrub send to the AI, and can I turn it off?

When you upload slides, the text of the file goes to our AI provider to write your questions. When you ask Scrub something, your question goes with your stage, states and saved programs. Nothing is sent until you allow it, and it is not used to train AI models.

Turn it on or off any time under You, then Scrub and AI questions.

How do I change my states or specialties?

Open You and tap Restart onboarding. You answer the questions again and your board, notes and practice are kept.

Delete my account and everything in it

Open You, then Account & sign-in, then Delete account. There is no retention dark pattern here and no phone call to sit through. The deletion commitment itself, including how long the minimum tax and fraud records are kept, is written in the privacy policy rather than summarised here. We keep one copy of a promise, in the document that makes it.
